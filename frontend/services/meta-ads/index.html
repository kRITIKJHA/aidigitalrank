Common questions

How is this different from a typical Meta Ads agency?

Most agencies optimise for ROAS and stop there. We tie every decision back to your actual profit per order — factoring in COD, returns and discounts — so a "good ROAS" campaign that's quietly unprofitable gets caught and fixed.

Do you need access to our store data, not just Meta?

Yes. We compare what Meta reports against your actual Shopify orders, because ad-platform numbers alone can overstate what's really working.

What's the minimum budget to work with you?

It depends on your average order value and margins. We'll tell you honestly during the audit whether your current budget is ready to scale, or what needs to change first.

Curious what's working and what isn't in your account?

A short audit is usually enough to tell you if it's a targeting problem, a creative problem, or a numbers problem.
