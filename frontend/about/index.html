Our story

AIDigitalRank started with a simple frustration: too many businesses were being sold vanity metrics dressed up as strategy — big reach numbers, high CTRs, campaigns that "performed well" on a dashboard but never quite showed up in the bank account.

We built AIDigitalRank around one idea: marketing should be judged the same way any other business decision is — by the return it generates, not by how it looks on a slide.

100% of our recommendations are tied back to a number you can verify in your own store data
